# NOTES.md

Scratch space. **Not a decision record.** Anything here is non-authoritative; only `DECISIONS.md` is binding.

Things that go here:
- Open questions to chew on between sessions
- Empirical observations from training runs that haven't yet earned a `DECISIONS.md` line
- Reminders to self
- Half-formed alternatives that lost a coin flip

Things that do **not** go here:
- Architectural decisions (→ `DECISIONS.md` + `CHANGELOG.md`)
- Constants (→ `constants.py`)
- Per-session activity (→ `docs/sessions/`)
